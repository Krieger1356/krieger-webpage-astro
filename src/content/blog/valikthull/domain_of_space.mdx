## An Ciúnas Mór Information
![Cathair nan Lasair](/images/dosp.webp)

The Domain of Space is a paradox of contained infinity. It is a swirling, miniature galaxy of amethyst and azure light, held in perfect stasis within a translucent sphere of arcane power. Beyond this fragile shell churns the raw, chaotic magic of Valik’thull, making the domain a pocket of impossible, ordered beauty adrift in a sea of chaos. Its stars are not balls of gas, but luminous, inhabited settlements—villages, towns, and cities—whose size and population grow with their star’s brightness. All orbit in silent harmony under the Prince’s rule, with only a few militant outposts breaking the pervasive peace.

At the heart of this contained cosmos rests the **Talla Sholas nan Reultan—the Hall of Starlight**. It is an inverted tower, a spike of impossible architecture whose broad base is anchored to the very inner skin of the domain’s spherical boundary, while its needle-like pinnacle stretches down towards the galactic core. It exists in a pocket of perpetual, soft twilight and possesses its own localized gravity, allowing one to walk its walls as if they were floors.

The tower itself is a masterpiece of glittering amethyst crystal. Access between its many levels is granted by an ethereal platform elevator that moves not by lever or button, but by the sheer intent of its passenger. Its primary chambers are few but vast: a library containing shelves that stretch into perceptual infinity, holding an unknown quantity of universal knowledge; a workshop humming with magical arrays and intricate tools for shaping reality; and, at the very bottom—or top—of the inverted structure, the penthouse.

This penthouse is the private sanctum of **Prince Cathigan Aonorach**. Encased by a flawless glass dome, it offers a breathtaking, 360-degree panorama of the entire swirling galaxy under his care. Richly furnished and centered around a luxurious bed, with an attached bathhouse of celestial design, it is a throne room without a throne. Here, the Prince of Space looks not down upon subjects, but out upon a universe contained within his grasp, a silent sovereign in a hall of perpetual starlight.

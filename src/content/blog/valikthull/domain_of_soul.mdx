## Anam Information
[![soul_domain.png](https://i.postimg.cc/50syw47b/soul_domain.png)](https://postimg.cc/mzFT4sW5)

The Domain of Soul, Anam, is a shattered realm of chilling beauty. Its landscape is carved from vast, broken plains of pearlescent obsidian that glint with a thousand captive colors: deep violets, venomous greens, and the crimson of old wounds. The air is cold and heavy, an oppressive silence broken only by the whisper of fear against stone. For the obsidian does not merely reflect light; it reflects the soul. Those who gaze too long into its depths see their own terrors staring back.

A relentless military presence patrols these mirrored wastes, a silent ward against intrusion. At the heart of this desolate splendor lies the domain’s core and its cage: Snàth Damhain-Allaidh (Spider’s Silk), a vast, softly glowing white sphere. Here resides Sylas Ceannas, the Prince of Soul. His citizens cluster in its shadow or within its labyrinthine halls, living by one unspoken rule: navigate your days carefully, and never, ever catch the eye of the spider at the center of the web.

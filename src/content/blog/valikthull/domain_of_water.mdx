## Uisce Information
![Water Domain](/images/dowa.webp)

The Domain of Water is a self-contained spherical ocean where permanent land doesn’t exist—only temporary islands of floating coral and volcanic rock that rise and sink with the tides. Weather defines entire regions simultaneously: one quadrant may be a glassy mirror of calm, while another rages with monsoons, hurricanes, and twisting water spouts. Inhabitants live in floating cities—organic amalgamations of thousands of vessels, from ancient wooden ships to living coral structures, connected by rope bridges and flowing waterways that serve as streets. They’re a lively, adventurous people whose lives revolve around fishing the abundant seas and trading through shimmering portals to other Domains. The oceans teem with life, from shimmering schools of fish to massive beasts lurking in the abyssal depths.

At the domain’s deepest point lies the Pale Jewel of the Deep—a massive aquamarine pyramid where nobles from major floating cities gather yearly in its central chamber. The lower levels contain the prince’s chambers, perpetually empty, for the ruler of this watery world prefers wandering its ever-changing surface to sitting on any throne.
